**Organization > Branding** replaces the Samuori logo in two places with your organization's own: the portal top
bar and the sign-in page. It is logo only. Samuori's colours, the rest of its artwork, the `NOTICE`, the licence and
the About dialog are unchanged, and the About dialog still identifies Samuori.

## What to upload

A PNG or SVG image, up to 512 KB. A transparent background is recommended so the logo sits on the top bar and the
sign-in card without a box around it. The top bar is a wide strip, so a logo about three to four times as wide as it
is tall fits best; a square or tall logo still works but shows smaller.

When you choose a file, the page reports its format, size, dimensions and aspect ratio, whether it has a transparent
background, and shows it in a mock top bar and sign-in card so you can see how it looks before saving.

## Light and dark

The top bar and sign-in page follow each viewer's light or dark theme. A single logo is used for both. If your logo
is dark and would be hard to see on the dark top bar, the page suggests adding a separate **dark-mode logo**; it is
used only on dark surfaces, and the main logo is used everywhere else.

## Changing and removing

Saving replaces the current logo. **Reset to Samuori logo** removes it and reverts to the Samuori mark everywhere;
removing the main logo also removes the dark-mode one. **Remove dark-mode logo** leaves the main logo in place. Only
organization administrators can set, change or remove the logo.

SVG files are sanitized on the server before they are stored or shown: scripts, event handlers and links to other
sites are removed, so an uploaded SVG cannot run code or load anything from elsewhere.
